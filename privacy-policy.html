1. Overview

Welcome to SG Digital Poster, developed and operated by Sumit Groups. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our application.

SG Digital Poster is a digital content creation app that helps individuals and businesses create, customize, and share professional poster designs for festivals, promotions, social media, and business branding.

By installing or using SG Digital Poster, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app and discontinue use.

This policy applies to all users of the SG Digital Poster Android application (package: com.sgdigitalposter.app) available on the Google Play Store.

📦

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our services:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you register or log in via Google, Facebook, or WhatsApp.
  • Business Profile: Business name, logo, address, contact details, and social media links that you add to your business card/profile.
  • User-Created Content: Posters, designs, images, and text that you create within the app.
  • Payment Details: Billing name, address, and payment method (processed securely via third-party gateways — we do not store card numbers).
  • Support Queries: Messages or emails you send to our support team.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID), and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Features used, screens visited, session duration, crash logs, and app performance data.
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile network carrier, and connection type (Wi-Fi / mobile data).
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID for serving personalized advertisements via AdMob.
  • Push Notification Token: Device token used to send push notifications via OneSignal.

C. Information from Third-Party Login

  • Google Sign-In: Name, email, profile photo, and Google account ID.
  • Facebook Login: Name, email, profile photo, and Facebook user ID.
  • WhatsApp Login: Phone number used for authentication.

6. Payments & Billing

SG Digital Poster offers premium subscriptions and one-time purchases for access to advanced templates, features, and poster categories.

  • All payment transactions are processed by our certified third-party payment gateways (Razorpay, Paytm, PhonePe, Cashfree, Stripe).
  • We do not store your full card number, CVV, or net-banking credentials on our servers.
  • Payment gateways are PCI-DSS compliant and use industry-standard encryption.
  • Transaction records (amount, date, subscription type) are stored to manage your subscription status.
  • Refund requests are subject to our Refund Policy. Contact support within 7 days of purchase for eligible refunds.
⚠️ Subscription Auto-Renewal: Premium subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage subscriptions via Google Play → Subscriptions.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data security is our priority. Here is how we protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive user data is stored on secured servers with access controls and regular security audits.
  • Firebase Security: User authentication tokens and database access are protected by Firebase Security Rules.
  • Local Storage: Designed posters saved locally on your device are stored in your private app directory.
  • Data Retention: We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 10).
  • Server Location: Data is primarily stored on Google Firebase servers (Mumbai/Asia-South1 region).
Despite our best efforts, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your device secure.

8. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@sgdigitalposter.com. We will promptly delete the information from our records.
